Help & Contact

Masjid Times · congregation times for masjids across Ontario

Found a wrong time? Open the masjid in the app and tap Report a wrong time. For anything else — a missing masjid, a bug, or a question — send us a message.

Where do the times come from?

Iqamah (congregation) times are read every day from each masjid's own website or prayer-time screen. Adhan times are calculated on your phone for the masjid's location. Every masjid shows when its times were last checked, and links to its own website.

A time doesn't match my masjid's board

Masjids change their times through the year, sometimes before their website does. Always trust the masjid itself, and please report the difference so it can be fixed for everyone.

Why does Asr differ between masjids?

Masjids follow either the standard (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) or the Hanafi calculation for Asr, which can be about an hour later. Settings → Asr calculation lets you choose how it is shown.

My masjid is missing

Send us its name and website and we'll add it once we can read its times.

Location and privacy

The app works without your location; you can pick a neighbourhood instead. See the privacy policy for what the app stores and why.

Deleting your account

Accounts are optional. If you made one, go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the app.
