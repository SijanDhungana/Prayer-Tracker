Privacy Policy

Masjid Times · Effective 1 October 2026

The short version. Masjid Times shows congregation (iqamah) times for masjids near you. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. You don't need an account, and nothing about you is sold or shared for advertising.

Kept on your device

Your settings (Asr calculation, theme, clock format), favourite and home masjids, the neighbourhood you measure distances from, and a copy of the latest prayer times are saved in the app's storage on your phone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.

Your location

The app asks for your location only when you tap Use my location, and it works fully without it. Your location is used on your device to measure how far each masjid is; it is not sent to us or stored on a server.

If you use Plan a trip, the start and destination you enter, or your current location if you choose it, are sent to Google Maps Platform to find addresses, routes and drive times. Google handles that information under the Google Privacy Policy.

Optional accounts

An account is only needed to suggest a corrected prayer time. If you create one, your email address and password are handled by Supabase, our account provider, and any corrections you suggest (the masjid, the time and your note) are stored with your account so they can be reviewed. When a correction is approved it becomes part of the public timetable, without your name or email.

Deleting your account: in the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. Your account and your suggestions are deleted immediately.

When the app downloads prayer times

The app fetches the latest times from our website, which is hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, it may keep standard request logs (such as IP address and time) for a short period for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify anyone.

Who else is involved

Only the service providers named above: Supabase (accounts), Google Maps Platform (maps and trip planning) and Vercel (hosting). We don't sell personal information or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Children

Masjid Times is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. An account isn't needed to use the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
